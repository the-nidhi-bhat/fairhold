# FairHold three-minute demo

1. Sign in with a buyer identity, open the dashboard and explain that demo
   identities are signed but are not production authentication.
2. Create a deal and add milestones with amounts and acceptance criteria.
3. Sign in as the seller, submit evidence for one milestone and explain that
   evidence review is separate from payment approval.
4. Review as the buyer, record the deterministic evidence recommendation, and
   approve or reject it. Payment operations require a human decision.
5. Show payment history and explicit hold/capture/void state. Explain that an
   authorization is not escrow or a capture.
6. Return to the dashboard and distinguish simulated, pending and real sandbox
   authorization counts.
