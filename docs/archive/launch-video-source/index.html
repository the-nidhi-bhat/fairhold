01 / The problem

Work ships. Payment stalls.

Scope drifts, approval is verbal, and the money moves before anyone agrees it should.

02 / The product

FairHold

Milestone payments for work where both sides need proof.

  • Authorization hold
  • Evidence
  • Human approval
  • Provider capture

03 / Five actors

Five actors. One separation of duties.

  • 01 AI proposes Drafts milestones, reads evidence, writes advisory notes
  • 02 Rules decide Transitions are gated in code, not by whoever asks loudest
  • 03 Humans approve A named participant accepts the evidence for that milestone
  • 04 PayPal executes The provider moves the authorized amount after approval
  • 05 The audit trail records everything Every action, decision and provider outcome

No single one can spend.

09 / Execute

Then the provider moves the money.

01

Reserve the operation

One intent, one idempotency key.

02

Capture the exact amount

$4,200.00, not a rounded total.

03

Record the result

Completed, pending and failed are different facts.

A timeout is not a failure: the operation stays pending until it is reconciled. A duplicate capture is refused, not repeated.

Provider call simulated in this build.

10 / Record

Every fact gets a line.

  • 14:02 Authorization created recorded
  • 14:02 Evidence submitted recorded
  • 14:09 Evidence accepted recorded
  • 14:09 Human approval recorded recorded
  • 14:10 Capture completed recorded
  • 14:11 capture_pending — kept pending, not failed pending
  • 14:12 Unknown event — stored, never applied quarantined

A webhook that does not match is quarantined, not guessed.
